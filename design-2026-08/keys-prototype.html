Reading a lead sheet

TEACH3 of 7

A lead sheet gives you the melody and the chord symbols, and nothing else. Your left hand decides what the harmony sounds like. Start by playing only the root of each symbol on beat one, then add the rest of the chord once the changes feel automatic.

  1. Play the melody alone, right hand, one time through.
  2. Say each chord symbol out loud on beat one.
  3. Add the root in the left hand, nothing else.
  4. Fill in the full chord under the melody.
Both chords landed on the beat. Keep going.

No video for this step. The written steps and the keyboard are the whole lesson.

Why Fmaj7 sits under that melody

The melody note is E, the seventh of F. Playing a plain F triad under it leaves the E unsupported, so the arrangement uses Fmaj7 and the note becomes part of the chord instead of a passing tone.
