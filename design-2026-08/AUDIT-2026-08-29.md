## What the redesign delivered

- The design IS the app markup: 20 artboards extracted to committed source,
  rendered verbatim, data bound in. No colour, size, radius or spacing is
  written anywhere in the port.
- Desktop composition 1418x738 that fills Mark's window, plus the 756 column
  below that width. Legacy top bar absorbed everywhere under the canon; every
  screen carries the design's own Library control instead.
- All tools drawer (7b): 17 tools, three columns, descriptions, filter, legend
  in shape and word.
- Tabs switch the table, search filters across every arrangement live, sort
  persists, quests stick, real album art at two shipped sizes.
- The deck implements artboard 8a end to end: breathing strike line, continuous
  nearness (r 14+26n^2, a 0.18+0.82n^3), strike ring 18 to 64px/180ms, chord
  window sharing one ring with dimmed cores, 2.2Hz sustain pulse, tier ambient
  wash from tier 3, spark budget 60.
- Accessibility ported (aria/roles carried over, every control named, focus
  ring in the accent), true rollback (`window.__restorePreCanon()`), service
  worker v60 that never caches a failure.

## What is NOT done, said plainly

1. **No P-45 session.** Machine gates cannot judge feel, latency at the keys,
   or whether the deck reads at speed. This is the acceptance test and it is
   Mark's.
2. **Codex's critical-path matrix** is only a third built: MIDI
   connect/disconnect mid-play, settlement idempotence run twice, narrow
   viewport walk, and isolated snapshots per journey are still missing.
3. **Sub-1418 windows get the 756 column with the interim tools list**, not a
   designed narrow drawer. Fine on the laptop, undesigned on a half-screen.
4. **Gap B controls** (play transport, metronome fields, echo/calibrate
   buttons) work but were never drawn as artboards; they inherit the canon look
   through tokens only.
5. **The freeze offer** state is drawn (6q) but unbound under the canon.
6. The deck spec's scanline tail texture on held pills is approximated by the
   pulse, not the literal 6px scrolling scanline.

Honest read: Keys beats everything above on ownership, offline, scoring
honesty, accessibility and (now) visual identity. It loses on library breadth
and iOS, both by design: curated arrangements were a council ruling against the
importer tarpit, and iOS lacks Web MIDI entirely.

## Verdict on the design itself

The strongest thing about it is restraint with one bright moment: true black,
hairline structure, light belonging to the notes. It looks like a product, not
a template, and nothing else in this market commits to OLED black or to
colour-blind law. Weakest points: the 756 column wastes mid-size windows, and
Gap B screens are one visual notch behind the drawn ones. Both are known,
recorded, and design work rather than port work.
