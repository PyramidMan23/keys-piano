    Improv playground

    No judgement zone. Nothing here is scored, ever. Pick a loop, start the backing, and make noise over it: bright filled-dot keys are the CURRENT chord's tones, outlined-dot keys are safe scale tones.

    Takes

    Your piano's action is not uniform and neither is your hand. Play the asked key at the asked strength; the app learns YOUR soft, medium and strong per register, and all dynamics feedback maps through that instead of raw numbers.
