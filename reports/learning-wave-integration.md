# Learning and theory wave integration

The six packages from the transcript audit are connected to the existing app. This report supersedes the earlier package reports' historical "not wired yet" notes. Final verification and delivery state are in `learning-wave-acceptance.md`.

| Area | Implemented behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Reading | Separate guided learning and first-reading modes; preview, chosen tempo, count-in and pulse; persistent exposure receipts; separate pitch, rhythm and continuity results; honest help/retry/partial-scope credit and preserved earlier progress. |
| Written rhythm | Authored notation, tap/hold and note-release assessment for durations, rests, dots, ties, 3/4, 6/8 and triplets. Existing ear-copy rhythm remains available. Repair targets the selected bar. |
| Notation | Flats, naturals, key signatures, accidental rules, ledger notes, changing positions, interval/look-ahead and aligned two-hand reading. LabScore draws the authored spelling, rests, ties, slurs, tuplets and expression marks. |
| Applied theory | Four verified catalogue passages and an original cadence study connect harmony and inversions to actual playing. Unsupported catalogue annotations are refused rather than guessed. |
| Technique and expression | Technique detours preserve the actual passage, range, tempo, hand and help settings, including reload recovery. Touch, dynamics, balance and pedal activities distinguish measured input from listening self-assessment. |
| Route and rewards | Configurable reading/skill/passage/enjoyment route; skippable and reorderable. Twenty-nine cards progress through demonstration, guided work, independent work, later recall and transfer. Practice, reading and repertoire evidence remain distinct. |

There are 146 base stages and 50 alternate transfer exercises. New material is finite: exhausted pools remain useful practice and stop claiming novelty. Assisted performances can succeed as practice without filling independent, recall or transfer badges. Guided passage success uses actual played-note evidence, because the repertoire engine's weighted score for flawless wait-mode input is 80.

Independent Opus 5 reviews covered reading, curriculum and UI behaviour. Codex verified findings and supervised fixes, including retry guards, passage attribution, one-bar repair, authored accidental rendering, valid self-assessment, animation cancellation and saved-result reconstruction. The existing library, collections, song tiers, score cards, seek/restart, guide and immersion work was retained and merged with upstream v132 history.

The new surfaces use the app's existing typography, cream notation panels, dark palette and mint actions. Longer worked-example guidance is available under an accessible disclosure; the primary worked action is visible at 390px without shrinking the score. Keyboard and reduced-motion support remain in place.

No learner efficacy claim follows from simulated tests. A real P-45 session and actual retention over time remain human acceptance work. The app cannot infer gaze, posture, fingering or artistic intent from MIDI.
