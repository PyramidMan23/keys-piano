# Keys practice surface

Keys is Mark's personal piano learning app, used with his Yamaha P-45.
The existing canonical artboards and CLAUDE.md govern its visual language.
This extension follows the acceptance and design contract in BUILD-FIVE.md.

## Experience

Open a piece, see one useful next step, and keep the piano and music dominant.
Practice any chosen passage, with repeatable settings and honest progress evidence.
Keep the guide collapsible and immersion reachable with a double click.
On narrow screens the guide starts collapsed but remains directly recoverable.

## Visual contract

Retain the existing dark green chrome, Fraunces titles, restrained mint actions,
album artwork and cream notation page. New disclosures share the existing
controls, spacing and type hierarchy. Avoid adding competing dashboards.

## Quality bar

- Current Falls/Score selection must match the visible surface.
- All new controls must support keyboard focus and fit a 390px viewport.
- Readable notation takes priority over fitting every measure onto the screen.
- Unknown rhythm stays explicitly labeled as a pitch guide.
- Source comparisons disclose coverage and disagreement; they do not imply editorial approval.
- A passage loop never earns whole-song mastery. Comparisons require matching
  passage, scope, hands, tempo and help settings.
- Restart and tempo changes preserve the learner's selected passage.

Physical keyboard feel and actual learning require a real learner session.
Browser simulation verifies behavior, not retention or musical interpretation.
